# Concept Review Checklist

Review the blind packet before provenance or AI recommendations. Confirm `anonymous-candidates.jsonl` was frozen before Anchor reveal.

- Does the core show one repeatable multi-feature visual grammar?
- Does variation remain inside that grammar across subjects and contexts?
- Is the result still recognizable after source-specific motifs are ignored?
- Are boundary sides independent and free of shared evidence, near duplicates, contexts, and source groups?
- For parent/child claims, does the broad side exclude the child and honestly represent the remaining parent breadth?
- Do the distinguishing rules describe positive visible differences?
- Does the proposed Anchor level match the evidence: parent, work leaf, series, movement, technique, or community label?
- Is this useful as a separate Planner choice and a separate generation recipe?
- Does the live official catalog captured for this run already contain the same identity, an alias, a parent, or a near-duplicate recipe?
- For a close official match, would the candidate produce a meaningfully different Planner choice and generation recipe? If not, it should not advance.

AI pre-populates one outcome per hypothesis: advance, merge, split, hold, or reject. Review the batch, edit only exceptions, and use the concept-stage approval as acceptance of unchanged proposals. Record parent/child, related, and alias separately as relations. State what new evidence or test would resolve a hold.
